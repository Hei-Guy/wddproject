Overview

Purpose

To transform mountain biking videos into an organized learning resource where everyday riders explore trails by location and skill level, understand trail difficulty, and gain confidence.

Audience

Beginner to intermediate mountain bikers and local Eastern Idaho riders seeking approachable trail guidance, realistic terrain previews, and practical gear.
